Project completed by Navya Penati + Amanda Nicholson + Logan Roberts

I contributed to the robot project mainly by focusing on the robot’s design and wiring. I helped develop the overall design strategy for the robot, including how it would collect cubes and stay within the competition constraints. Our robot was designed to be fully autonomous and collect as many cubes as possible during the one-minute competition by moving toward the cube area and keeping cubes within its perimeter.

I also worked on wiring the robot and helping connect the electrical components needed for movement and sensing. Throughout the project, I helped troubleshoot issues with the robot, especially when parts of the design or wiring affected the sensors. Overall, this project helped me learn more about the connection between mechanical design, wiring, and reliable robot performance during a competition.
